| OQ-120 | What is the per-subscription credit ceiling, and what happens to a grant above it? | `MAX_CREDITS` is 1,048,576 per subscription. | OQ-121 | After `hello{resume}`, do credits restart at zero? | `hello` carries `lastSeq` per sid but no credit. | OQ-122 | What does `TopicDef.ring` bound, and how long do delivered lossless items stay for resume? | The library reads `ring {items, bytes}` as the bound of one subscription's outbox. | OQ-123 | What does `seq` count? | Every item the producer offers to a subscription gets the next seq (1, 2, ...). The contract only says `seq` is a non-negative integer. | The UI client's gap detection (M0-49) is written against it. |
| OQ-124 | Which Problem codes and frames answer the protocol limits? | The 65th subscription and an oversized snapshot end the subscription with `end{reason: error}` and `BUDGET_EXCEEDED` (429 and 413); a subscription during `goaway` gets `ENGINE_SHUTTING_DOWN` (503).
