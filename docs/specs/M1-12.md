# M1-12 Peek planner, pure, covering all b.8 rows

**Issue:** #97 · **Milestone:** M1 · **Wave:** M1-W2 · **Size:** L; six slices with hard budgets of about 800 changed lines each, tests included (see "Slices") · **Owner area:** peek · **trust:critical:** yes. A wrong answer here makes the broker drop or dead-letter a user's message. CODEOWNERS already lists `/libs/shared/peek-planner/`; no protected path is touched.

Status: working spec, agile mode (working defaults, every assumption a row of `docs/open-questions.md`, "Spec questions: M1-12", ids OQ-1421 and up). The approval stop of `/wp-spec` is lifted by the maintainer for this work package. Written at `96cacdc`. Dependencies of the issue: #86 M1-01, #88 M1-03, #42 M0-46 landed; #53 M0-62 has not (decision below).

## Goal

A new pure library `libs/shared/peek-planner` with one entry point, `planPeek(req, facts)` (also `peekPlanner.plan`), that turns the semantics registry, the preflight, the broker profile and the principal into either a `PeekPlanDraft` (method, caps, hold budget, guard, mandatory disposition, expected effects, warnings, consents) or a `PeekRejection` with a closed code. It plans a **side-effect-aware peek**: it never says a classic-queue or quorum-queue read is "non-destructive" or "read-only" (AGENTS.md section 1).

Serves: MSG-02, MSG-05 planning; DC-06, DC-07, DC-08, DC-09, DC-10, DC-13, DC-14, DC-16; ADR-008, ADR-028; amendments **A-1** (doom guard, disposition freshness), **A-2** (DLX-source evidence, finite-unknown L) and **A-3** (HTTP fallback). Invariants I-3 (no broker version is compared; every era fact is asked of the registry), I-13 (nothing is dropped silently: every clamp and every conservative substitution is in the answer). Consumers that come later: M1-13 (the preflight that fills the input), M1-14 and M1-15 (executor, guard, dispositions), M1-16 (the `messages.peek` action).

## Decision: does the planner need M0-62? No

M0-62 delivers the matrix generator and the lane evidence: the generated matrix document, the conformance lanes and the status flips. The planner needs none of them. It reads the registry through its public lookups (`cell`, `isDoomed`, `peekCaps`, `warning`), and the registry exists on `main` with the cells, caps and warnings of b.2 and b.8 (M0-12, M1-01). What it cannot know is **how far the lab has confirmed a cell**, and it must not claim more than the registry says. So:

- every cell the planner uses is `code-derived`; the preview carries `semanticsVersion` and a `confidence` of `code-derived` (or `unverified-version` when the broker is not verified), never `lab-verified`;
- when M0-62 lands and a cell flips, the planner needs no change (the status is data), only its receipts show the stronger confidence (M1-16 reads the status from the cell id).

Logged as OQ-1421. Nothing in this work package waits for M0-62.

## Rules

**R1 Pure and total.** No I/O, no clock (`facts.nowMs` is an input), no randomness. The input is never mutated (the specs deep-freeze it). Every output list is sorted (warnings by id, consents, expected cells by id). A `SemanticsGapError` is caught and becomes a conservative path (R12); any other exception from a registry is a bug and propagates. A well-typed input gives a value, never a throw. The library imports only `@app/contracts`, `@app/domain-model`, `@app/semantics` and `@app/routing` (types), all `type:pure`/`type:contracts`; it does not import `Principal` from `@app/ports` (a pure library may not) and declares the structural fields it reads.

**R2 Evaluation order is the contract.** The first rejection wins and later rules are not evaluated:

1. request valid (`count` a positive safe integer, `maxBytes` the same when present, the preflight numbers finite) else `INVALID_REQUEST`;
2. the profile's gate for `messages.peek`, when the profile carries one and it is disabled: `GATE`;
3. a stream: `STREAM_QUEUE` (read by offset, never `basic.get`; W-STREAM);
4. an exclusive queue owned by another connection: `EXCLUSIVE` (W-EXCLUSIVE);
5. a broker that is not `verified` and a non-human principal: `UNVERIFIED_BROKER` (R12);
6. a quorum queue whose `quorumOk` is not `true`: `NO_QUORUM` (fail closed: absent is "not checked");
7. a quorum queue with single active consumer and an active consumer: `SAC_ACTIVE`;
8. the settle row for the queue's limit state (R3): no row at all is `SEMANTICS_GAP` for everyone;
9. the nack cell is destructive for this limit state (3.13 with `-1`): `LIMIT_MINUS1_313`;
10. the DLX hazard of R7 says `blocked`: `OVERLIMIT_DLQ` with W-OVERLIMIT and the fix;
11. a non-human principal and any substituted row (R12): `SEMANTICS_GAP` (this needs the rows of step 10, so it comes after it);
12. the hold budget (R5) leaves no room for one message: `BUDGET_TOO_SMALL`;
13. the method (R9) is not available: `METHOD_UNAVAILABLE`;
14. an average message does not fit the byte cap (R4): `BUDGET_TOO_SMALL`; the registry has no caps row: `SEMANTICS_GAP`;
15. the disposition (R8): `NO_SAFE_DISPOSITION`, `RESCUE_PREFLIGHT_FAILED` or `INVALID_REQUEST`.

`env` is part of the facts for binding (a plan is bound to its environment class by the executor) but no decision here depends on it (OQ-1437).

**R3 The limit.** The preflight gives `deliveryLimit: DeliveryLimit` and `limitState: LimitState` (the raw configured form: `unset` and `minus1` are both "unlimited" to `DeliveryLimit` and differ on 3.13). `finite` pairs with `finite`, `unlimited` with `unset` or `minus1`; `unknown` pairs with anything; any other pair is incoherent and is read as `unknown` with the reason `limit-incoherent`. A classic queue has no limit (basis `unlimited`, no cell is read). The planner's **basis** is one of `unlimited`, `finite`, `finite-unknown`:

- `unknown` own limit, or an R7 verdict of `unknown`, makes the basis `finite-unknown` (A-2);
- the cell is read for the limit state (`finite` stands in for `unknown`); the lookup tries the preflight's delayed-retry type first, then no delayed-retry key (eras whose cells do not have one), and uses `disabled` only when the type was not read, in which case W-DELAY is added as a consent (the type is unknown, so the delay is possible).

**R4 Caps.** `registry.peekCaps(era, queueType, role, verification)` with `role = admin` when `profile.identity.isAdmin`. The effective count is `min(requested, caps.max, hold count, byte count)`; `limitedBy` names the first binding bound in the order `cap`, `hold`, `bytes`, `request` (a clamp is reported, never silent: I-13, OQ-1432). The byte cap is `min(maxBytes ?? caps.maxBytes, caps.maxBytes)`; the byte count is `floor(bytes / avgBytes)` when `avgBytes` is known.

**R5 Hold budget.** `hardAbort = min(30 s, ttl/4, queue-timeout/4 | channel-timeout - 90 s)` where the timeout is `consumerTimeoutMs` or the broker default 1,800,000 ms (OQ-1431) and the formula is chosen by the `consumer_timeout` cell: a timeout that adds no failed delivery (`deltaDC` 0, the 4.3 return) is queue-level (divide by 4), one that does (the 406 channel close) is channel-level (subtract 90 s), no cell means both. (The first draft keyed on `countsTowardLimit: 'no'`; the cells show 3.13 with no limit also says `no` while its timeout still closes the channel, so `deltaDC` is the discriminator.) `target = min(5 s, hardAbort)`; `hold count = floor(target / rttMs)`. The answer lists the bounds that equal the hard abort in `derivedFrom` (`hard-cap`, `message-ttl`, `consumer-timeout`, `channel-timeout`). An `rttMs` that is not a positive finite number, `hold count < 1`, a hard abort `<= 0` or a byte count `< 1` is `BUDGET_TOO_SMALL` with the reason code.

**R6 Guard.** `counter` is `x-delivery-count` in every era; `doomedWhen` is `>` when `isDoomed(era, {deliveryCount: L}, finite L)` is false and `>=` when it is true (probe, L = the finite limit or 20); `stopAtFirstDoomed` is always `true`. The limit is the queue's finite L, `unlimited`, or, for `finite-unknown`, `finite min(20, every visible finite limit)` with `estimated: true` (A-2). A counter that cannot be read is doomed (that is the executor's job, M1-15; the planner states the rule in the preview and does not weaken it).

**R7 The DLX hazard (W-OVERLIMIT, A-2, DC-07).** Only for a quorum target whose basis is not `unlimited` and only in an era whose doom rule is strict (`carries`, from the probe; OQ-1422). The evidence is `preflight.dlxSources` (the `sources`, `complete` and `unknownReasons` of `dlxSources` / `routing.dlx-sources`) and `preflight.heldDeathSources` (queue names from the x-death of messages the preflight has seen, M1-04).

- Every name of `heldDeathSources` that is not among the sources is added as a source with an `unknown` limit (A-2: a queue holding broker x-death entries is a DLX target; the planner does not trust the caller to have merged it). `heldDeathSources` absent means "not looked at": it does not change the verdict, but the answer says `heldDeath: 'not-checked'` and R9 uses it.
- A source's worst arrival is `Ls + 1` (limit exceeded) plus the +1 of one failed delivery (`close_while_holding`, W-CRASH), read from the registry cell (one when the cell is a gap). The queue is **blocked** when `isDoomed(era, {deliveryCount: worst}, Lt)` for any source with a finite limit, or when a source is `unlimited` (a count with no ceiling; OQ-1423). With the strict rule this is `Lt <= Ls + 1`, so the minimum safe finite limit is `max(Ls) + 2`, and `-1` is always safe.
- Otherwise the verdict is **unknown** when `complete` is false, when any source limit is `unknown`, or when the target limit is unknown; else **safe**. An empty source list is "no hazard" **only** when `complete` is true (A-2).
- `blocked` is `OVERLIMIT_DLQ` with W-OVERLIMIT and the fix `{kind: 'delivery-limit-policy', queue, limit: -1, minimumFinite: n | null}` (`null` when a source is unlimited). `unknown` is a human consent (`W-DLX-UNVERIFIED`), makes the basis `finite-unknown`, and blocks AI and unattended principals (R8).

**R8 Disposition (DC-09, A-1, A-2).** Mandatory when the queue is quorum and the basis is not `unlimited`; classic and unlimited queues get `{kind: 'none'}`.

- `quarantine`: needs `preflight.rescue` with `queueName`, `queueReady`, `overflowSafe`, `permissionOk`, no alarm, publishing not blocked; otherwise `RESCUE_PREFLIGHT_FAILED` listing the failed checks. For a non-human principal the rescue must be at most 60 s old (`nowMs - rescue.checkedAt`) (A-1).
- `raise-limit`: broker administrators only (`profile.identity.isAdmin`); unavailable when the basis is `finite-unknown` (A-2), when `limitFixedBy` says an argument or an operator policy fixes the limit (A-4), or when the policy name starts with `nsb.` or names a provider-owned object of the profile; the value is `-1` only if the registry's `minus1` nack cell is non-destructive and keeps the head position (3.13: destructive; 4.0-4.2: tail), else a finite integer above the current finite limit. A non-human principal needs a preflight at most 60 s old.
- `let-broker-handle`: human interactive principals only, `typedConsent` equal to the queue name, adds the consent `disposition:let-broker-handle`. It is never offered, accepted or defaulted for AI or unattended principals (DC-09).
- No disposition requested: `quarantine` when its rescue passes (`chosenBy: 'default'`), else `NO_SAFE_DISPOSITION`; the rejection lists `offers: ['let-broker-handle']` for a human. For a non-human principal with `finite-unknown` the rejection is `NO_SAFE_DISPOSITION` with the reason `limit-unknown` or `dlx-evidence-unverified` whatever was requested (A-2: blocked).

**R9 Method (DC-10, A-3).** Default `amqp091.get`. A single-active-consumer queue with no active consumer: classic keeps `get` (W-SAC-CQ); quorum uses `amqp091.consumer-sac` (consent W-SAC-QQ) only when the `consumer_timeout` cell adds no failed delivery (`deltaDC` 0, not `countsTowardLimit: 'yes'`, not destructive), else `METHOD_UNAVAILABLE`. `prefer: 'amqp10-step'` is offered for quorum queues without SAC when the `amqp10.released` cell passes the same test **and** the doom rule is strict (the 1.0 header shows the counter the guard needs); otherwise `METHOD_UNAVAILABLE`. `prefer: 'http-fallback'` (A-3) is offered only for a classic queue, or for a quorum queue that is **unlimited, provably non-DLX** (`complete`, no source, `heldDeathSources` present) and in a strict-rule era; never for a finite or unknown limit, never for a queue that fails the DLX check, never in an era without the strict rule. Its count is capped at 50. The list is exhaustive (OQ-1433).

**R10 Warnings.** Always from `registry.warning(id)` (a missing id is a gap, R12), never prose. Triggers: W-BUDGET (consent): nack cell counts `yes` and AC+1. W-313 (consent): nack cell DC+1 and no AC. W-TAIL: position `tail`. W-DELAY (consent): position `delayed`. W-OVERLIMIT: only in the rejection. W-REDELIVERED, W-HOLD, W-CRASH: every `get` plan. W-DROPHEAD (consent): `drop-head` overflow with a `maxLength` and `ready + count >= 90 %` (an unknown `ready` counts as a hit). W-TTL: a TTL is set. W-SAC-CQ, W-SAC-QQ: R9. W-EXCLUSIVE, W-STREAM: only in their rejections. W-UPGRADED: `limitDisagreement` present. W-MIXED: `profile.versions.mixed`. The planner-owned warnings (OQ-1425, texts in `libs/shared/peek-planner/i18n/en/warnings.json`): `W-DLX-UNVERIFIED` (consent), `W-LIMIT-UNKNOWN` (consent), `W-BROKER-UNVERIFIED` (consent), `W-SEMANTICS-GAP` (consent). A warning of severity `consent` adds its id to `consentsRequired`.

**R11 Principals.** A principal is **human interactive** when it, and every principal it acts for, has kind in `desktop-user`, `local-token`, `team-user`, `api-token`, surface in `ui`, `cli` and `unattended: false`. Anything else (an MCP client, a job, the system, an unattended token) is **non-human** (OQ-1426).

**R12 Gaps and unverified builds.** A cell lookup that throws `SemanticsGapError` is retried in this order: the same era with limit state `finite`; the conservative era (`4.0-4.2`, as `semantics.md` says and as `caps.ts` does: a typed constant used as a lookup key, never compared) with the limit state; the conservative era with `finite`. If no row answers, `SEMANTICS_GAP`. A substituted row adds `W-SEMANTICS-GAP` (consent) and the preview lists the missing keys in `gaps`; a non-human principal is `SEMANTICS_GAP`-rejected whenever `gaps` is not empty. A profile that is not `verified` adds `W-BROKER-UNVERIFIED` (consent) and the registry caps are already conservative; a non-human principal is rejected (`UNVERIFIED_BROKER`).

**Interfaces** (the types are abridged; `types.ts` is authoritative)

```ts
export function planPeek(req: PeekRequest, facts: PeekFacts): PeekPlanDraft | PeekRejection;
export const peekPlanner: { plan: typeof planPeek };

interface PeekFacts {
  profile: Pick<BrokerProfile, 'era' | 'verification' | 'versions' | 'identity' | 'managedObjects' | 'gates'>;
  preflight: PeekPreflight; principal: PeekPrincipal;
  registry: PlannerRegistry; // Pick<BrokerSemanticsRegistry, 'version' | 'cell' | 'isDoomed' | 'peekCaps' | 'warning'>
  env: EnvClass; nowMs: number;
}
interface PeekPreflight {
  queue: {
    type: QueueType; exclusiveOwnedByOther: boolean; autoDelete: boolean;
    deliveryLimit: DeliveryLimit; limitState: LimitState;
    limitDisagreement?: { stat: DeliveryLimit; computed: DeliveryLimit };
    limitFixedBy?: 'argument' | 'operator-policy' | null;
    visibleLimits?: readonly number[];
    singleActiveConsumer: boolean; hasActiveConsumer: boolean; consumers: number;
    ready?: number; quorumOk?: boolean; avgBytes?: number;
    delayedRetry?: { type: 'disabled' | 'all' | 'failed' | 'returned'; minMs?: number; messagesDelayed?: number };
    maxLength?: number; overflow?: 'drop-head' | 'reject-publish' | 'reject-publish-dlx'; ttlMs?: number; consumerTimeoutMs?: number;
  };
  dlxSources: Pick<DlxSourcesResult, 'sources' | 'complete' | 'unknownReasons'>; // as returned by routing.dlx-sources
  heldDeathSources?: readonly string[];
  rescue?: { queueName: string; queueReady: boolean; overflowSafe: boolean; permissionOk: boolean; alarms: readonly string[]; publishBlocked: boolean; checkedAt: number };
  rttMs: number; takenAt: number;
}
interface PeekPlanDraft { preview: PeekPlanPreview; gates: PlanGate[]; warnings: WarningRef[]; consentsRequired: string[] }
interface PeekRejection { rejected: true; code: RejectionCode; textKey: string; textVersion: 1; reasons: ReasonCode[]; warnings: WarningRef[]; fix?: SuggestedFix; offers?: Disposition['kind'][] }
```

`PeekPlanDraft` is structurally a kernel `PlanDraft<PeekPlanPreview>`; `gates` is always empty (blocks are rejections; the field exists for the executor, OQ-1435). `PeekPlanPreview` adds to the interface of `core-interfaces.md`: `semanticsVersion`, `confidence`, `queueType`, `limit {basis, value?, estimated, reasons}`, `caps.{requested, limitedBy}`, `evidence {dlx, heldDeath}`, `gaps`, and `honestLabelKey` in place of the prose `honestLabel`.

## What M1-13 (the preflight) must pass

`limitState` next to `deliveryLimit` (the registry keys cells by it; 3.13 `-1` and unset differ); `singleActiveConsumer` and `hasActiveConsumer` separately; `dlxSources` exactly as `routing.dlx-sources` answered it, **with `complete` and `unknownReasons`**, and `heldDeathSources` from the death-history normaliser (M1-04) of messages it has seen, so that "x-death was looked at" and "it was not" differ (OQ-1407 of M1-20); `rescue.queueName` (the planner may not invent `<product>.quarantine`); `limitFixedBy`; `visibleLimits`; and `rttMs`. A field the preflight cannot fill is left **absent**, never defaulted: the planner reads absence as unknown.
