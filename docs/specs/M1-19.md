# M1-19 Publish engine: publish action and history (PUB-01/02 engine)

**Issue:** #104 · **Milestone:** M1 · **Wave:** M1-W2 · **Size:** M by label; five slices with budgets (see "Slices") · **Owner area:** engine / messages · **trust:critical:** no. A publish is a mutation, so it goes through the `ActionExecutor` (I-2), but the paths are not on the trust-critical list and no protected path is touched (see Risks).

Status: draft for human approval (written with `/wp-spec`). Nothing is implemented. Every question is a row in `docs/open-questions.md`, section "Spec questions: M1-19", ids OQ-1438 and up, each with the default this spec implements.

## Goal

The engine side of the publish studio (PUB-01) and its history (PUB-02): an action `messages.publish` that sends N copies of one message on a dedicated `publish` lease (confirm channel, always `mandatory`) and answers with an honest outcome for each copy (`confirmed`, `nacked`, `returned`, and the cases the port also has), and a bounded, per-owner, per-connection history of what was published, kept 30 days, from which a publish can be sent again with its secrets masked. Serves PUB-01, PUB-02, DC-12 (separate publish connection, confirms and `mandatory`), A-9 (the lease is the one place a `ConfirmedCopyProof` is minted), ADR-009, ADR-014 (field encryption) and the invariants I-2, I-6, I-7, I-10 and I-13.

The UI (the Monaco publish studio, M1-46), bulk publish (PUB-04), templates (PUB-03) and AMQP 1.0 publish (PUB-06) are not part of this work package.

## Rules

**R1 The publish funnel (I-2, I-4 untouched).** `messages.publish` is `kind: 'command'`, `audit: 'attempt+outcome'`, `risk: 'medium'`, `planning: 'optional'` (the guardrail policy raises both in prod and staging, and the prod lock applies; an unarmed prod connection is refused with `GATE_BLOCKED` before any lease is taken). It has `plan()` (reads nothing from the broker; the preview is the exchange, the key, the copy count, the byte size and the header count; the body travels as the plan's `sensitivePayload`, encrypted at rest, purged with the plan) and `revalidate()` (returns `valid`: there is no broker state it depends on). `execute` takes the lease from `ctx.writes.connections.publish(cid, vhost, ctx.principal, ctx.signal)` and nothing else.

**R2 One lease, sequential copies, always released.** One `PublishLease` per call. Copy `i` is sent after copy `i-1` has an outcome. `lease.release()` runs exactly once on every path (success, thrown error, abort, deadline). `mandatory: true` is passed on every call; the input cannot turn it off, and neither `confirm` (OQ-1438).

**R3 Outcomes are what the lease said, nothing better.** A copy is `confirmed` only when the lease returned `outcome: 'confirmed'`. `returned` (the broker found no queue) is never a success, even though the broker also confirms; `allConfirmed` is true only when every copy is `confirmed`. `timeout` means "no confirm in time"; the text says the broker may still have routed the message, and the action never turns it into `confirmed` or into `nacked`. `blocked` means nothing was sent for that copy. The output has no proof object and nothing that is not plain JSON.

**R4 When the loop stops (no silent loss, I-13).** After `blocked` or `timeout` the loop stops (the lease abandons its channel after a timeout, and a blocked connection will not take the next copy); the remaining copies are listed as `not-sent`. A `nacked` or `returned` copy does not stop the loop: the next copy is independent. An aborted `ctx.signal` or a passed deadline stops it the same way. Every copy of `copies` appears in the output exactly once.

**R5 Errors.** If the lease cannot be taken, or the first call throws before any copy has an outcome, nothing was sent: the action throws a Problem (`AppError`) whose code is the adapter's `code` when it is one of the contract `ErrorCode`s (`NOT_FOUND` for a missing exchange, `FORBIDDEN_BROKER`, `BROKER_RESOURCE_LOCKED`, `BROKER_UNREACHABLE`, ...) and `INTERNAL` otherwise. If a later copy throws, the copies already decided stay in the output, that copy is `failed` with the code as `reason`, and the rest are `not-sent`. No Problem, reason or log line holds a body, a header value, a property value or a key (I-10); the `detail` is the adapter's safe `detail` or a fixed text.

**R6 Bounds (I-6, I-7).** At most 100 copies, a body of at most 1 MiB decoded, at most 64 headers nested at most 8 levels, strings at most 255 characters where AMQP says so, a per-copy wait of 100 to 30,000 ms. An input over a bound is `VALIDATION_FAILED` before any lease is taken. Nothing is buffered: copies are not queued, there is one in flight. Bulk publish (many messages, templates, a generator) is PUB-04 and is not built here.

## History rules

**H1 What is recorded.** After the loop, one history row per call that put at least one copy on the wire (the first copy had an outcome). A call that threw before any outcome records nothing (R5). Recording happens inside `execute`, after the outcomes are known, and never changes them: if the row cannot be written, `historyRecorded` is false and the failure is logged without values (AC-12, AC-13).

**H2 What is stored.** Plain columns: id, owner (the principal's id), connection id, vhost, time, `expiresAt` (`at` + 30 days), exchange, routing key, copy count, the per-outcome counts, body size and sha256. One sealed document (`FieldSealer`, table `publish_history`, column `data_sealed`, AAD from the row id): the properties, the headers and the body, **already masked** (H3). The unmasked values are never written, so there is nothing to unmask later (OQ-1442). A locked vault stores nothing: no plaintext fallback (I-10), the call gets `historyRecorded: false`.

**H3 Masking, applied before sealing and again on read.** Header and property values under a credential key (the kernel's list, at any depth) become `REDACTED`; a text body goes through `redactText` (credentialed URIs, secret query parameters, JWTs, bearer values) and, when it parses as JSON, `redactValue` (credential keys at any depth). The list `masked` names every place a value was replaced. A body that is not valid UTF-8 text, or is over 64 KiB, is not stored: `bodyOmitted` says why and the size and sha256 stay. The mask can miss a secret that has no recognisable shape (a bare token in free text); that is a stated limit, not a guarantee, and the studio says that a re-send is a starting point, not a copy (OQ-1442).

**H4 Scope.** Every read takes the owner from the principal. An entry of another owner, an entry of another connection and an unknown id all answer `NOT_FOUND` (no existence leak). `list` is a keyset window of at most 200 entries, newest first, filtered by connection (required), vhost, exchange, routing-key prefix and a time range; there is no search over bodies in this work package (OQ-1442). Grouping by day is the UI's job.

**H5 Retention.** 30 days, a constant `PUBLISH_HISTORY_RETENTION_MS` (OQ-1443 asks whether it is a setting). `expiresAt <= now` is expired. Purge runs at most `PURGE_BATCH` (200) rows at a time, on every write and when the module starts; an expired row is also never returned by `list` or `get` even before it is purged. There is no timer.

## Risks / questions

Every question has a default that this spec implements; each is a row of `docs/open-questions.md` ("Spec questions: M1-19", OQ-1438 to OQ-1450). Agile mode: none needs an answer before the first manual test, but please read OQ-1438, OQ-1442, OQ-1445 and OQ-1446 first.

- **OQ-1438 mandatory and confirm as inputs.** The issue's interface has `mandatory` and `confirm` as flags. `PublishLease` accepts only `mandatory: true` and has confirm channels only (DC-12). Default: both fields exist in the contract as `true` only; `false` is refused. Switching either off would need a port change and a decision on DC-12.
- **OQ-1439 outcome set.** The issue names three outcomes; the port has five. Default: all five plus `failed` (a later copy threw) and `not-sent` (the loop stopped), so no copy is missing from the output. `timeout` is worded as "no confirm in time; the broker may still have routed it".
- **OQ-1440 no registry row.** The issue assumes `NO_ROUTE` and returns behave as the matrix says; the registry has no row. Default: none is read or added; the outcome is what the lease reported on that broker, and no behaviour is claimed beyond it (I-3, I-14). A lab cell for mandatory returns across versions is a separate `/new-semantics-cell` package if wanted.
- **OQ-1441 the scripted lease in `svc-messages`.** Building a `confirmed` outcome needs `mintConfirmedCopyProof`, which only a publish lease may import (A-9). Default: `fake-publish.ts` is a test-only file in `svc-messages`; if the architecture test refuses it, the fake moves to `libs/testkit` (allowed to build leases) and the specs import it from there.
- **OQ-1442 history stores the masked form, not the original.** "Returned for re-sending with secrets masked" can mean "mask on read of a sealed original" or "never store the secret". Default: mask before sealing, so a stolen database never holds a secret the mask caught; the cost is that a re-send cannot restore a masked value, and a secret with no recognisable shape can slip through the mask (stated in the UI by M1-46). No body search; binary and over-64-KiB bodies are not kept.
- **OQ-1443 retention and purge.** 30 days from the issue; blueprint 6.3 gives no value for publish history and nothing for team mode. Default: a constant for all modes, purged on write and at start, bounded to 200 rows per pass, no timer. A setting, a team value and a scheduled job are follow-ups.
- **OQ-1444 ids, permission, MCP.** Default ids `messages.publish`, `messages.publish-history.list`, `messages.publish-history.get` (fallback `messages.history-*` if the generator refuses three parts); history reads use the catalogue permission `messages.publish` (no history permission exists); `--mcp=none`.
- **OQ-1445 registering `svc-messages` in the engine.** Adding the contracts makes `compose-services.spec.ts` AC-1 fail unless the ids are served or listed in `INTENTIONALLY_UNSERVED`. Default: register the module in `coreModules` (a small, additive edit of the composition root); `messages.publish` then answers `CAPABILITY_MISSING` in `engine:serve` until the manager is wired. The alternative is three `INTENTIONALLY_UNSERVED` entries.
- **OQ-1446 wiring the ConnectionManager.** Out of scope here (OQ-357, OQ-771). Please confirm that a publish that cannot run in the composed engine yet is acceptable for this work package, or name the package that wires it first (it also blocks the peek action of M1-16 and the UI M1-46).
- **OQ-1447 adapter errors.** OQ-210 (mapping `Amqp091Error`) is open. Default: the action reads `code` and `detail` structurally and maps to `AppError`; nothing is imported from the adapter.
- **OQ-1448 owner, delete, clear.** Owner is `principal.id`. No clear or delete action and no cascade when a connection profile is deleted; the 30-day rule removes the rows. A privacy-minded "clear history" is the obvious follow-up.
- **OQ-1449 migration number and OQ ids.** `0011` is the next free number on `5269954` (`origin/main`); the worktree of M1-23 or another package may take it first, in which case this one renumbers when it rebases (the file name and the id inside it change together). OQ-1438 and up were free on every worktree checked on 2026-10-26; renumber the same way if they collide.
- **OQ-1450 limits.** 100 copies, 1 MiB body, 64 headers, depth 8, wait 100 ms to 30 s (default 5 s). Chosen as a bounded single publish; PUB-04 will need others. The lease's own maximum wait is 600 s.
- **Protected paths.** None needed. No dependency, no `pnpm-workspace.yaml` change, no allow-list change, no CODEOWNERS line (the project is not `trust:critical`; if the maintainer wants the funnel path owned, the proposed text is `/libs/engine/svc-messages/src/actions/messages-publish.ts @RafaelJCamara`, for a maintainer to apply). No lab evidence is written.
- **ADR.** No new ADR proposed: ADR-014 covers the sealed history, DC-12 covers the lease. If the maintainer wants the "mask before storing" rule (OQ-1442) recorded as a decision, it is one ADR line for a maintainer to approve.
- **Uncertainty.** The nack in AC-20 depends on how the broker applies `reject-publish` to a publish with `mandatory`; it is expected, not verified. It is the lab's to confirm.

## As built, slice A (2026-10-26)

- **AC-9.** The prod lock refuses an unarmed call before the audit `attempt` row is written, so the unarmed case has no audit row. The row above is corrected.

- **Scripted lease.** It lives inside `messages-publish.spec.ts`, not in a `fake-publish.ts`: a non-spec file that names a lease type breaks the I-2 funnel rule, and `mintConfirmedCopyProof` is allowed only in specs. The OQ-1441 fallback did not apply.
- **Dependencies of the action.** `deps.ts` has a `recorder` (`PublishRecorder`) and a `logger`. The history repository and sealer arrive with slices B and C, and slices C and D implement the recorder.
- **Test engine.** `createTestEngine` gained an optional `writes` option. `compose-services.spec.ts` lists `messages.publish` as intentionally unserved; slice D removes that entry.

## As built, slice B (2026-10-26)

Slice B (`PublishHistoryRepository` in `@app/ports`, migration `0011_publish_history`, the repository for both dialects, the contract suite) differs from the text above in these ways. Slice C builds on what the code does.

- **Columns and cursor.** The time column is `published_at`, not `at`. The window cursor is `<published_at>.<id>`.
- **Window and get.** `window` returns `PublishHistoryHead` rows without the sealed data; `get` returns the full `PublishHistoryRow` with it (I-6, I-7).
- **Expiry.** `PublishHistoryQuery` carries a required `now`, so `window` filters expired rows in SQL. `get` does not filter expiry: the row exposes `expiresAt` and the service (slice C) must check it.
- **Routing-key prefix.** It uses `substr(routing_key, 1, n) = prefix`, not `LIKE`, so it is case-sensitive and treats wildcards literally on both dialects.
- **Bounds.** The repository throws `RangeError` for a window limit outside 1..200, a purge limit outside 1..1000, a bad cursor, and a sealed document over 1 MiB (`PUBLISH_HISTORY_MAX_SEALED_BYTES`). The three constants are exported from `@app/ports`.
- **AC-18** is one named describe (11 cases) run by the SQLite spec and by `persistence.it.ts` on SQLite and Postgres 16/18, not a single `it`; `publish_history` is in its name so `--testNamePattern=publish_history` selects it.
- **Postgres.** Not run in the slice (no Docker daemon); the first CI run of `persistence:it` with Postgres confirms it.

## As built, slice C (2026-10-26)

Slice C (`mask.ts`, `publish-history.ts`, a memory repository; AC-11 to AC-16) differs from the text above in these ways. Slice D builds on what the code does.

- **Shape.** `createPublishHistory({ history, sealer, newId, clock, logger })` returns a `PublishHistory` that is a `PublishRecorder` (`record`) plus `window`, `get` and `purge`. Slice D passes it to the action as `recorder` and builds the two history actions on `window` and `get`; the module-start purge is `purge()`. The types `HistorySummary`, `ResendData`, `HistoryEntry`, `HistoryQuery` and `HistoryWindow` are in `internal/publish-history.ts`; slice D's contracts must match them. `HistorySummary` adds `bodySha256` (the spec says "size and sha256 in the summary") and has `summary` as the seven per-outcome counts.
- **Memory repository.** There was none; `createMemoryPublishHistory()` is new in `src/testing.ts`, with `failInserts`, `seed`, `dump` and `sealedOf` for specs. It follows the rules of the contract suite (ordering, cursor, expiry in `window` only, case-sensitive prefix, `RangeError` bounds) but is not run against that suite.
- **AC-13 and AC-14 run the action.** The action of slice A, with the store as its recorder, runs over a scripted lease inside `publish-history.spec.ts` (the lease lives in the spec for the reason given for slice A). Slice D therefore only has to wire the store, not extend these tests.
- **Errors.** A store or sealer failure becomes a fixed text with no `cause`: `record` gives `GATE_BLOCKED` for a locked vault, `VALIDATION_FAILED` for a document over the repository's 1 MiB sealed limit (64 headers of 60,000 characters pass the contract and do not fit) and `INTERNAL` for a store failure; `get` gives `NOT_FOUND` (one text for another owner, another connection, an unknown id and an expired entry), `INTERNAL` for a document that does not open or is not a history document (the vault may be locked, or the entry damaged; the sealer cannot tell which); `window` gives `VALIDATION_FAILED` for a limit outside 1 to 200 and for a cursor the repository refuses.
- **Mask.** (1) A run of more than 1,024 non-space characters becomes `[long]` before the kernel's expressions run: they take 1.5 to 3.5 s on 64 KiB of one run (OQ-1481). (2) The text rules are the kernel's plus the two of the capture projection (`name=value`, Basic and Digest values), exported from `projection.ts` as `redactNamedCredentials`. (3) A JSON body is rewritten only when something was masked, so a body with nothing to mask keeps its layout and its numbers as typed; the walk stops at 32 levels (a body can nest far deeper than the stack). (4) Bytes headers that read as text are held to the text rules. (5) Properties are masked too (`prop:<name>`), which the spec's list of places lacked.
- **Read.** `get` runs the mask again on the opened document, so an entry written under older rules cannot hand back a secret; the places reported on write stay in `masked`.
- **Purge.** `record` purges up to 200 expired rows after the insert, and a purge failure is logged with a fixed text and does not fail the write. The retention is `PUBLISH_HISTORY_RETENTION_MS` from the publish time given in the record.
- **Not changed.** No port, migration, contract, action, `deps.ts` or `apps/engine` file. `MessagesModuleDeps` still has `recorder` and `logger`; slice D replaces `recorder` with the ports the store needs and builds the store in `createMessagesModule`.
- **Mutation pass.** 48 hand-made mutants of the two files (expiry comparison, retention, purge batch and call, limit bounds, window filters, every mask rule and bound, remask, dedupe, the JSON unchanged test) were run against the two specs; five survived the first pass (limit message, property key, property masking before sealing, depth cut) and each now has a test that kills it.
