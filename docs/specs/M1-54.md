## Goal

The person sees how a broker is behaving over time, cheaply and honestly: **live charts** of the cluster (message backlog, publish/deliver/ack rates, consumers, memory and disk per node) on the cluster overview page, and of one queue (depth, ready and unacked, consumers) on the entity page's **Charts** tab, with a **forecast overlay** (the engine's drain ETA with its 95 % band) and a **Forecast** tab that says every state in words (an ETA, reached, moving away, no reliable forecast, insufficient data). History comes from `metrics.query`, new points from the `metrics.live` topic into a bounded client ring. A series the engine does not hold is "not reported", never zero; every gap, stale feed, fallback, approximation and drop the engine tells is said next to the chart (I-13). Read-only inventory-style work: no broker-changing action is called.

Serves: MON-02 (live charts), MON-05 (forecast, UI half), DC-31 (gates and reasons as sent), DC-32/DC-33 (the engine paces collection; the UI only asks for demand), ADR-021, ADR-034; invariants I-1 (the UI reads through `EngineApi` and the live client only), I-6 (the client ring is bounded by count and bytes, drops are counted and shown), I-7 (at most 50 series per query, one queue per page), I-10 (nothing secret exists here), I-13 (no silent loss). Blueprint sections 8 and 10.

## Rules

**R1 Honest series.** Each line of a chart is one of: **drawn** (it has points), **not reported** (`found: false`, or no point yet: listed in the legend with its reason, no line, no zero), **hidden** (the person toggled it). A value is never invented, interpolated across a gap, or shown as `0` because it is missing. A gap in time longer than three times the series' median interval breaks the line (a visible gap, not a bridge).

**R2 Bounded ring (I-6, I-13).** One ring per series: at most **1,000 points and 16,000 bytes** (two `float64` per point). A point whose time is not newer than the last is ignored and counted (`stale`); a full ring evicts the oldest and counts (`evicted`). Pushed = kept + evicted + stale. The session holds at most 50 rings (the engine's query limit). Counts are shown ("N points dropped by this view") whenever above zero.

**R3 Session.** `createMetricsSession(data, {cid, series, queues, clock})` follows the live topic **first**, buffers what arrives, asks `metrics.query` for history, merges (history points older than the first live point first, then live, by time, no duplicates), then follows. States: `loading` (no answer yet), `ready` (at least one series drawn), `empty` (answered, no series has a point: every series is "not reported", the engine's status explains), `error` (the history read failed: the Problem as received, the live part kept if it works), and for the live part `connecting | following | ended` with the Problem when it ended (the rings keep what they hold). A `gap` or `reset` from the live client asks history again once (the gap is counted, not hidden). `pause()` freezes the **view** (rings keep receiving, bounded; the number received while paused is shown), `resume()` shows the current rings. `reload()` reads history again. `destroy()` aborts and unsubscribes. No timer: the session is driven by events; the page throttles redraws with a frame scheduler.

**R4 Gap explanations** (`explainStatus`, data in the model). From the engine's `status`, in fixed codes the UI words: `no-source` (source `none`: the engine has neither Prometheus nor management statistics to read), `loading` (no round has finished), `stale` (newest point older than three intervals, with `asOf`), `unavailable`, `fallback-no-endpoint`, `fallback-failing`, `approximate`, each `partial` reason (`load-balancer-fronted`, `nodes-without-endpoint`, `per-object-unavailable`, `queue-demand-truncated`, `targets-unreachable`, `fallback`), `dropped-points`, `dropped-series`, `dropped-samples` (counts), `series-not-reported` (per series), and `unknown-state` for any value this version does not know (never silent, never "fine"). The text of each is a Transloco key; the engine's code is on the element as `data-code`.

**R5 Forecast view** (`forecastView`). The five states become a view model: `ok` (ETA duration, the time of the ETA, band low and high or "no upper bound", rate per second, R2 as a percentage, the window and point count), `reached` ("the backlog is empty"), `moving-away` with its reason (`flat`, `away`, `beyond-horizon`), `no-reliable-forecast` (R2 and window, **never an ETA**), `insufficient-data` (points and span against what is needed). Any other state, or an `ok` whose numbers are not finite or whose band is not ordered, is shown as "forecast unavailable" (fail closed). The overlay is drawn only for `ok`.

**R6 Drawing.** Pure functions in `libs/ui/charts`: nice ticks (1, 2, 5 times a power of ten), time ticks aligned to a step of 1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 30 s, 1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 30 min, 1 h; a linear scale; the **y-scale choice**: `zero` (from 0 to the nice maximum), `fit` (from the nice minimum to the nice maximum, a flat series gets a padded range, never a zero-height axis) and `log` (a signed `log10(1 + |v|)`, so zero is drawn and negative values are not lost; ticks at 0 and powers of ten); a **min-max-first-last (M4) downsample** to the pixel width (at most four points per pixel column, keeps every spike); path strings with gap breaks and coordinates rounded to one decimal; non-finite values never reach a path. The forecast overlay is a segment from the newest point along the engine's rate to the zero line, a band across the plot between `lowMs` and `highMs` after the newest point (to the right edge when `highMs` is `null`), clipped to the plot, and a marker line at the ETA; an ETA beyond the extended domain is said in text, not drawn.

**R7 Accessibility.** Every chart's plot is a horizontal `slider` over the points of its first visible series (named by the chart title, described by the key help; the pattern for scrubbing along a time axis, so the keys are the platform's own): Left/Right move the cursor one point, PageUp/PageDown ten, Home/End to the first and last, Escape clears; the **readout** (time, then each visible series and its value) is the slider's value text, so it is announced on keyboard moves only (a pointer hover shows the same text in a box without changing what is announced); a **summary** live region says the newest values and the state, updated at most once per 15 s of data time and on state changes; every chart has a **data table alternative** (a real `<table>` in a `<details>`: time column, one column per series, newest first, at most 100 rows, the rest counted); the legend buttons are `aria-pressed` toggles; colour is never the only signal (each series has a dash pattern and a marker shape); the palette is `--ds-color-chart-1..6`, at least 3:1 on every surface in every theme and distinguishable under protan, deutan and tritan simulation; **no animation or transition** is used (a stale or live chart never moves by itself, and pause exists), so `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured by construction; pointer targets are at least `--ds-target-size`.

**R8 Page states** (each in words, polite `role="status"`, none by colour alone): no connection (nothing, no call); never probed (the probe prompt, no call); loading; empty ("not reported" for each series with the engine's reasons); error (the Problem as sent, its hint by code, Retry); live ended (the Problem, the data kept); stale (`asOf`); paused ("N new points waiting"); and every R4 explanation present. The Charts and Forecast tabs and the overview section call only `metrics.query`, `metrics.forecast` and subscribe `metrics.live`.

**R9 Ranges and controls.** Range 5 min, 15 min (default), 30 min, 1 h is a **view filter over the ring** (no new request); the ring holds the whole hot window. Pause/Resume, y-scale (`zero`, `fit`, `log`), series toggles and Refresh are plain buttons or a labelled select with a visible text. The selected range and scale are kept per page, not stored.

**R10 Forecast cadence.** The forecast is read when the tab opens, on Refresh, and every 30 s while the page is open and not paused (a timer token, replaced by a manual one in specs); an in-flight read is never doubled; a failure keeps the last answer, marked earlier, and says the Problem.

## What is only proved against fakes

Everything in the feature runs over the model's fake `MetricsData` or the real `EngineApi` and `LiveClient` over a `FakeEngine` that answers `metrics.query` and `metrics.forecast` from fixtures and serves `metrics.live` through the real live protocol: the shapes are the contracts' (parsed in test builds), the data are fixtures, not a broker's. Not proved: a real collector round (the engine's own specs prove that), real cadence and clock skew, jsdom has no layout (widths are inputs; the pixel result of the SVG is not looked at), the renderer CPU of 50 charts in a browser, colour appearance on a real display, screen reader behaviour (roles and names are asserted, speech is not), and the three themes beyond the token checks. The maintainer should look at all of these by hand.

## Risks / questions

Each is a working default recorded in `docs/open-questions.md`, "Spec questions: M1-54".

- **Q-1 No charting library; SVG written locally.** The issue names uPlot 1.6.32; a new package is a protected change and the maintainer's instruction forbids it. The draw maths are pure and the components small, so a library can replace the renderer later.
- **Q-2 No `feature-metrics` library.** The Charts tab and the overview belong to `feature-explorer`; a feature cannot import a feature. Shared parts are `ui-charts` and `metrics-model`.
- **Q-3 Counters stay raw in the ring; rates are derived on the client.** Because `metrics.live` carries raw counter values.
- **Q-4 The range selector filters the ring** (the hot hour); longer ranges are listed disabled until the persisted store (M4-04).
- **Q-5 Queue charts are for queues, not streams.** The metrics catalogue's queue series come from queue metrics; a stream's tab set is unchanged (Charts and Forecast stay unlisted there).
- **Q-6 Forecast cadence 30 s**, read through the action, not computed client-side.
- **Q-7 Summary live region throttle 15 s of data time.**
- **Q-8 The chart palette lives in `ui-design` tokens** (six colours, three themes), added to the token spec's checks.
- **Q-9 The overview Trends section appears for every connection** (a connection with no statistics shows its explanations); it is additive and loaded lazily after the overview.
- **Q-10 Per-queue rates are not charted** (the catalogue has none); needs an engine change (follow-up note).
- Estimated changed lines across the five slices: about 2,240, hard budgets 3,250, against the 800 guidance, which is why the work is five slices; `oversize-approved` is not requested.
